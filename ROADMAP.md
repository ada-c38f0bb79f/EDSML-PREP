## Completion order

1. Finish the compulsory Imperial Python lectures and Week 1 mathematics.
2. Build confidence with labelled environmental data in Week 2.
3. Implement numerical methods from first principles in Week 3.
4. Build a tested forward PDE solver in Week 4.
5. Reuse the forward solver inside an inverse problem in Week 5.
6. Compare statistical and machine-learning baselines in Week 6.
7. Compare a PINN against the trusted Week 4 finite-difference reference.
8. Select, clean, document, and present the strongest work in Week 8.

Official external courses are linked rather than copied wholesale. Several are
hundreds of megabytes or contain hundreds of notebooks. Each week includes a
small local starter artifact that is owned and completed in this repository.
